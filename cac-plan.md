## 1. One-sentence ICP (the filter for everything)

> **Solo freelance designers and developers (2–8 years in) who invoice through QuickBooks Online, have 5–20 active clients, and are stuck on day 8+ after sending an invoice — they've drafted follow-up emails and deleted them, need to get paid without sounding desperate, and have already tried manual Gmail nudges or QBO's generic reminders.**

### Four-part breakdown

| Part | GentleTap answer |
|------|------------------|
| **Buyer type** | Solo freelancer / independent consultant (design, dev, marketing) |
| **Context** | Uses QuickBooks Online + Gmail; bills $1.5k–$15k per project |
| **Pain this week** | One invoice is overdue; they keep postponing the follow-up email |
| **Already tried** | Manual "just checking in" emails, QuickBooks built-in reminders, calendar nudges |

### The “name 3 people” test

Before any new feature, ad, or landing page — can you name **3 real humans** who match the sentence above?

- If **no** → tighten ICP or talk to users until you can.
- If **yes** → every piece of content should sound like it was written for those 3 people only.

### What makes them convert

- Sees a **sample email** that sounds like something they'd actually send
- Realizes it **connects to QuickBooks** they already use
- **Free tier, no credit card** — low risk
- Setup under **5 minutes** with Gmail + QBO

### What makes them bounce

- Feels like debt-collection software
- Looks complicated to set up
- Doesn't trust the tone ("too corporate" or "too aggressive")
- Wrong accounting tool (FreshBooks/Xero) — not the primary ICP today

### One-liner (homepage + pitches)

> *The follow-up email you keep putting off — GentleTap sends it for you.*

## 2. SEO market research (June 2026)

### Market size & intent

Freelancers and micro-SMBs lose **~14 hours/week** chasing unpaid invoices (industry estimates). Dedicated AR automation is underserved below **$49/mo** for non-QB users, but **QuickBooks + Gmail freelancers** are a proven wedge — competitors like Paidnice built on top of accounting platforms because **built-in QBO reminders are generic**.

**Estimated monthly search demand (US/global English, third-party aggregates):**

| Keyword cluster | Est. volume | Difficulty | Priority |
|-----------------|-------------|------------|----------|
| accounts receivable software | ~8,100 | High | **Avoid** (enterprise) |
| overdue invoice | ~6,600 | Medium | Tier 2 |
| accounts receivable automation | ~4,400 | Medium-high | Tier 2 |
| automated payment reminders | ~3,200 | Medium | **Tier 1** |
| invoice reminder software | ~2,900 | Medium | **Tier 1** |
| QuickBooks payment reminders | ~500–2,000* | Low-medium | **Tier 1 — own this** |
| invoice follow up email template freelancer | ~200–800* | Low | **Tier 1 — own this** |
| how to follow up on overdue invoice | ~200–1,000* | Low | **Tier 1 — own this** |
| quickbooks send payment reminder | ~100–500* | Low | **Tier 1** |

\*Long-tail; exact volume varies by tool — high **intent** matters more than volume at this stage.

**Total addressable search** across AR/reminder terms: **38,000+/mo** — but GentleTap should win **narrow clusters**, not head terms.

### Competitor content map (what ranks today)

| Competitor | What they rank for | Gap GentleTap fills |
|------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| ChaseAI, ChaseBot, OwnedWork | Freelancer email templates | They sell templates/tools; **we automate from real QBO data + Gmail** |
| QuickBooks Intuit docs | How to turn on QBO reminders | Docs are procedural; **we compare + upgrade path** |
| Paidnice, Invoice Sherpa | Xero/QB AR automation | Often $29–49+, agency tone; **we're freelancer-voice, $19, free tier** |
| DEV.to / Reddit threads | "How I get clients to pay" | UGC ranks; **our guides + templates + product CTA** can compound |

### Positioning for SEO (not generic AR)

**Do rank for:** QuickBooks payment reminders · invoice follow-up templates · overdue invoice follow-up (freelancer) · automated payment reminders from Gmail

**Do not chase yet:** accounts receivable software · enterprise AR · Xero/FreshBooks (no integration = bounce)

### GEO / AI search (2026)

- Allow **GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot** in `robots.txt` — AI answers cite pages they can crawl.
- Use **FAQ + HowTo + SoftwareApplication** JSON-LD on every money page.
- Write definitional paragraphs AI can quote: *"GentleTap is automated invoice follow-up for freelancers using QuickBooks Online and Gmail."*

## 9. Messaging cheatsheet (use in titles, H1s, ads)

**Pain:** "Day 8 after the invoice. Still haven't followed up."  
**Promise:** "Polite payment reminders from your Gmail — stop when QuickBooks shows paid."  
**Proof:** Sample email preview on homepage.  
**CTA:** "Try free — no credit card · 5 invoices"  
**Avoid:** "Debt collection" · "AR automation platform" · "Enterprise"
